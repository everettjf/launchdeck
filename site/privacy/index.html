Privacy policy

Your launch history stays yours.

LaunchDeck does not include analytics, advertising, or accounts.

Data stored locally

Application indexes, favorites, folders, hidden apps, preferences, launch history, and up to 50 recent action records are stored on your Mac. Launch and action history can be cleared together in Settings.

Apple Intelligence

Optional semantic search uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models on supported Macs. Ordinary fuzzy search does not require Apple Intelligence.

No sale or sharing

LaunchDeck does not collect, sell, rent, or transmit personal data to the developer or third-party advertising services.

Questions

Contact xnuapp@gmail.com if you have a privacy question.
